Bhavya Talpada ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CanadaPR Chat mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the service:

DataHow It Is Collected
Email addressProvided during account registration / sign-in
Display nameEntered during profile setup
PR categorySelected during profile setup (e.g., Express Entry, PNP)
PR application monthSelected during profile setup
Chat messagesSent by you in community group chat rooms
Journey step datesEntered by you in the profile journey tracker

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate and improve the App:

  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Match you with the correct PR category and month-based group chats
  • Display your messages and profile to other community members
  • Allow you to track and record your PR journey milestones
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the community service.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on Supabase cloud infrastructure, which provides encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information. For Supabase's own data-processing details please see supabase.com/privacy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Supabase (authentication and database) – as described above.
  • Legal obligations: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Access – a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction – an update to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion – removal of your account and all associated data.

To exercise these rights, email us at support@canadaprchat.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
